How to play

Pick who the party is for, then let her play. Each line lights a candle; at the end she swipes the cake to blow them out and the family cheers (pick who comes under Coming to the party). Four steps: Magic (any key plays the next note), Follow (the song waits for the glowing key), Follow the friend (no glow: the friend on the right key hops and sings to call her; the glow comes back only if she needs it), By heart (nothing at first; a friend calls if she pauses). With Automatic on she moves up after two good songs and back if she gets stuck.
